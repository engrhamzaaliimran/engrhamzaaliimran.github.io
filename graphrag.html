08.1 · entity schema discovery

Let the corpus suggest the ontology, then review it manually

Candidate concepts were collected from the policy corpus, embedded with Nomic, projected with PCA, clustered with HDBSCAN, and manually reviewed into a domain-specific schema.

08.2.1 · interactive validation

Inspect every entity in its original context.

The standalone viewer contains the entire chunked corpus and allows filtering by source document, entity type, and confidence. It is used to check coverage, span boundaries, label quality, and the effect of moving the confidence threshold before relations are trusted.
